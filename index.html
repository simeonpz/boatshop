Services

One yard for the whole boat, from the keel up. If it is not listed here, ask. We probably do it.

Gelcoat restoration, buffing and waxing

Oxidised, chalky gelcoat brought back to a deep gloss, then sealed so it stays that way through the season. Colour matching and spot repair for chips and scratches.

Fiberglass and hull repair

Cracks, blisters, impact damage and soft spots, inspected honestly and repaired so the structure is sound, not just hidden.

Woodwork and varnishing

Brightwork stripped, faired and built back up coat by coat. Repairs to teak decks, rails, coamings and trim.

Engine service

Routine maintenance, repairs, spring commissioning and winterisation for outboards and inboards, so the engine starts when you need it.

Rigging

Standing and running rigging inspected, tuned and replaced. Mast stepping and unstepping at the start and end of the season.

Hauling and transport

Safe pickup and delivery to and from the yard, your mooring or storage. We can collect the boat, do the work and bring it back.

Meet Renan

I started RP Boatworks after more than twelve years working in Cape Cod boatyards, doing painting, fiberglass repair, woodwork, varnishing and just about everything else that keeps a boat in good shape.

I run the yard the way I would want my own boat looked after. I will tell you what actually needs doing, what can wait, and what it will cost, and then do the work properly. Most of our customers come back season after season, and most of the new ones are sent by them.

I live in Dennis with my wife and our two daughters, and you will usually find me in the yard. Call or send a message and we will work out what your boat needs.
